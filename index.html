ClearSource Temporary browser demo

Try the kiosk in your browser

Find official East Palestine documents, with sources. Ask a question, or plan a visit to the health district or the library.

About this demo. It runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere or saved. It uses the same safety rules and search as the Python app, on the same 7 short documents from 2023 and 2024. It is a class prototype and not for real health, safety or legal decisions.

Try:

Getting ready to talk to the health district, a state agency, or a librarian? Pick your situation and get one sheet: what the documents say, who to call, and what to ask.

My situation

A librarian can help you make any of these calls. Contact details came from public pages and still need to be verified.

What is the same as the Python app

  • The safety gate runs first. A health, safety, legal, blame or prompt-injection question gets a referral card, and search never runs.
  • Search is the same offline scoring over the same documents, with the same "not found" threshold.
  • Answers show the passage as written, the source, its date and an old-source warning.
  • The printable sheet text is the same. This demo was checked against the Python app on 25 questions and 20 sheets with no differences.

What this demo leaves out

  • The optional AI chat, the librarian note, the staff controls and the display settings.
  • Any saving or download. Use the Copy button to take a sheet with you.

What is not finished

  • The 7 documents are short excerpts from 2023 and 2024, and several are news reports quoting Ohio EPA, not agency documents.
  • Phone numbers and links on the referral cards still need to be verified.
  • The safety gate is keyword-based and has been tested on a small set of prompts.

Built by Jayant Jayakumar, Ohio University, for IEEE Tech4Good project 26-T4G-227, with Claude (Anthropic, Sonnet 5.5).

This shows what official documents say and who to contact. It does not say whether anything is safe or unsafe, and it is not medical, legal or environmental advice.
